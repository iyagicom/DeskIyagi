# Privacy Policy for DeskIyagi

**Last updated: October 21, 2026**

## Overview
DeskIyagi is a remote desktop and remote support tool.

## Data Collection
DeskIyagi does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer. Nothing is stored by the developer. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

## Network Use
To provide remote control, voice, file transfer and clipboard sync, the app connects to the device you choose to connect to. Session content is exchanged only between the participating devices.
